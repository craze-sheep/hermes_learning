# Minimum-Story Coverage: 55 IELTS Part 2 Topics → 6 Stories

Worked example using the 2026 May-August IELTS Part 2 topic bank (55 topics).
Stories based on the student's REAL autobiography for easier memorization.

## Key Lesson: Split Stories, Don't Force Connections

This mapping evolved from 3 stories → 6 stories after bullet-point verification
revealed forced connections. The user confirmed: "6个以内都可以" (up to 6 is fine).

**Initial attempt (3 stories):** 24 + 21 + 10 = 55 topics. But topics #15, #21,
#29, #40 had cue card bullets that couldn't be answered from the assigned stories.
#11 was also a stretch (friend "good at languages" didn't have specific methods).

**Final version (6 stories):** 7 + 9 + 8 + 17 + 9 + 5 = 55 topics. Every topic's
cue card bullets can be answered naturally. Only 4 topics need the user to prepare
one specific name/title each.

**Rule: When bullet-point verification fails, SPLIT the story rather than
rationalizing the connection.**

## Topics Needing Fabricated Specifics (4 topics) — RESOLVED

Only 4 out of 55 topics required the user to prepare one specific name/title.
These were resolved with globally-recognizable choices the examiner will know:

| # | 话题 | 选择 | 为什么选这个 |
|---|------|------|-------------|
| 7 | 最近看过的电视 | The Good Doctor | 全球热播，考官一定知道 |
| 15 | 名人广告 | 华为手机广告 | 华为全球知名，CS背景自然 |
| 21 | 拥有成功事业的人 | 西安开面馆的叔叔 | 和探亲故事融合，描述清楚即可 |
| 40 | 近期观影 | Oppenheimer | 奥斯卡最佳影片，科学家故事 |

**Principle:** Choose content the English-speaking examiner will recognize.
Avoid purely Chinese-specific content unless you describe it clearly enough
for a foreign listener to follow.
